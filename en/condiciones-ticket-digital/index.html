Legal and Tax Framework

Digital Receipt Issuance Terms

Information for diners and venues regarding tax validity, electronic consent, and digital safekeeping in accordance with current US regulations.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose and Legal Framework

These terms govern the generation, electronic issuance, delivery, and safekeeping of electronic simplified invoices (hereinafter, the "Digital Receipt") through the BarAI technological platform in participating hospitality venues.

The service is provided in strict compliance with:

  • Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Guidelines for electronic invoicing and record keeping.
  • Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA) and federal e-signature laws ensuring the validity of electronic records.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and other applicable state data protection laws.

2. Express Consent for Electronic Receipt

In accordance with electronic billing regulations, issuing invoices electronically requires the recipient's consent.

By voluntarily entering their email address on the table interface or requesting the bill electronically, the diner gives their express consent to receive the receipt in official PDF format sent to the provided address.

Right to request a paper copy: The customer retains the right at all times to request a printed paper copy from the venue staff if they so desire.

3. Requirements and Mandatory Receipt Content

Each Digital Receipt generated by BarAI has full legal and tax validity as a Simplified Invoice and contains:

Issuer Identification: Name or corporate name, Tax ID, and tax address of the hospitality establishment.
Unique Numbering: Unique sequential series and number of the simplified invoice.
Date and Time of Issuance: Timestamp of table checkout and payment.
Tax Breakdown: Subtotal, applicable tax rate, and total amount due.

4. Order Integrity Rule

To guarantee absolute transparency and protect consumer rights:

  • The Digital Receipt consolidates only those products that have reached the Completed status (served at the table).
  • Any canceled order or unserved item is automatically excluded from the final total with no charge to the customer.
  • The system issues a single official PDF document per table session, preventing duplicate charges or fragmented receipts.

5. Privacy and Email Usage

The email address provided by the diner:

• Exclusive purpose: It is used in a technical and automated manner exclusively for sending the digital receipt PDF and managing optional feedback.

• No unsolicited advertising: It will not be used to send newsletters, promotions, or commercial offers unless the user explicitly checks an additional consent box.

• No sharing: Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Requesting a Full Invoice with Tax ID

If a professional client or company requires a Full Ordinary Invoice (including their Tax ID, Corporate Name, and Address for tax deduction purposes), they may request it directly at the establishment by providing their tax details or by emailing the establishment and attaching the received digital receipt number.

7. Receipt Rectification and Claims

In the event of an error in the billed items, disagreement, or refund, the establishment will issue the corresponding rectifying invoice in accordance with tax legislation. Claims related to the service, product, or prices must be directed to the establishment that issued the receipt.

8. Ecological Commitment and Paperless

BarAI's Digital Receipt eliminates the use of thermal paper (often coated with non-recyclable BPS/BPA bisphenols), drastically reducing the carbon footprint, cellulose consumption, and toxic waste generation in the restaurant sector.
