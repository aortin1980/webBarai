Data Protection (CCPA & GDPR)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Controller

In accordance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we inform you that the controller for the personal data collected through this website is:

  • Owner: BarAI Technologies
  • Data Protection Email: info@barai.es
  • Primary Purpose: Management of restaurant software, support tickets, and delivery of digital receipts.

2. What data do we collect and why?

A. Contact & Early Access Form

We collect your name, venue name, phone, Tax ID, address, and email to manage commercial inquiries, set up product demos, and formalize service registrations.

B. Technical Support Tickets

We collect contact name, venue, email, and the technical issue description to provide assistance and track the ticket.

C. Digital Receipts for Diners

If a diner voluntarily enters their email to receive a PDF receipt, that email is used exclusively to send the consolidated payment receipt upon checkout. It is never sold to third parties or used for commercial spam.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

The legal basis for processing your data is:

  • User consent: By submitting any web form or requesting the digital receipt by email.
  • Execution of a contract: To provide the software service to our partner venues.
  • Legal compliance: For tax, accounting, and legal obligations.

4. Data Retention

Data will be kept only for the time strictly necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected and while legal or contractual obligations remain in force. Emails queued for digital receipts are deleted or anonymized once the PDF is delivered.

5. Your Rights (CCPA & GDPR)

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data processed by BarAI.
  • Request the rectification of inaccurate data.
  • Request its deletion (right to be forgotten) when no longer necessary.
  • Request the restriction or objection to processing.
  • Request the portability of your data.
  • Opt-out of the sale of your personal information (although we do not sell personal data).

You can exercise any of these rights by emailing info@barai.es with the subject "Data Protection" and attaching a copy of your ID.
