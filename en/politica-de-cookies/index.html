Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that websites download to your browser when you access certain pages. They allow a platform to remember user actions and preferences (such as login, language, font size, or active table order) so you don't have to configure them every time you return.

2. What types of cookies does BarAI use?

BarAI uses only necessary and functional cookies intended for the correct performance of hospitality operations:

A. Technical and Session Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These are essential for the application to function. They include the encrypted temporary token (client_token) that securely links a diner's orders to their table number while they are in the restaurant, and authentication cookies for waiters/chefs.

B. Performance and Anonymous Analytics Cookies

These collect aggregated, completely anonymous data about website traffic, load times, and tool usage rates to improve the technical stability of the service.

3. No Third-Party Advertising Cookies

BarAI does not use intrusive advertising tracking cookies or sell data to third-party ad networks. The platform prioritizes privacy, speed, and operational efficiency for customers and restaurants.

4. How to manage or disable Cookies?

You can allow, block, or delete the cookies installed on your device by configuring your browser options:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Safari (iOS / macOS): Preferences / Settings > Privacy > Block all cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Site permissions > Cookies and site data.

Note: If you block technical session cookies, you will not be able to send orders from your table as the system will not be able to securely identify your device to the POS.

5. Contact

For any questions about our cookie policy or data processing, you can contact us at info@barai.es.
