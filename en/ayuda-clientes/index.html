How does the PDF Digital Receipt work?

No more wrinkled thermal receipts that fade with heat or get lost in your pocket. BarAI revolutionizes digital invoicing for diners.

1. A single consolidated email

We won't flood your inbox with an email for every round. You enter your email just once, and the system groups all orders from your table when checking out.

Zero spam, 100% organized.

2. Legal Validity, Logo, and Tax

The attached PDF includes the venue's logo, official tax details, receipt number, and the breakdown of the taxable base and tax rate.

Ideal for justifying business expenses.

3. Only what you consumed

By strict security rules, the receipt only bills for products in the Completed / Served state. Any unserved dish is automatically canceled to protect you from improper charges.

Maximum transparency and trust.
